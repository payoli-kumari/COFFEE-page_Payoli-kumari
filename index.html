Our Story

Started with one espresso machine and a stubborn idea.

Ember & Bean began as a folding table and a two-group espresso machine at the end of this same block. We roasted small, poured slow, and figured if the coffee was honest, people would find their way in.

They did — and brought their appetites with them. So the menu grew: burgers off the flat-top, fries in small batches, and desserts built for sharing. Same corner, same care, a little more on the plate.

"We're not chasing trends. We're chasing the second cup you didn't plan on staying for." — Founders, Ember & Bean
